# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can ask to return items within {{return_window_days}} days after your order is delivered. Returns open only once the order shows as Delivered.

## How to return something
1. Open the order under Orders in your account and choose "Request a return".
2. Pick the items and quantities, and tell us why you are returning them (at least a few words).
3. We review the request. If it is approved, send the items back as instructed.
4. Once we receive the items, we issue the refund.
Return statuses: Requested, Approved, Received, Refunded, or Rejected.

## How much is refunded
You get back what you paid for the returned items, after any order discount, plus their share of the tax. The original shipping charge is not refunded.

## Where the money goes
Refunds go back the way you paid:
- Card payments are refunded to the same card. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it.
- Cash on delivery payments are refunded by us directly.
- Any part you paid with store credit goes back to your store credit straight away.
We may offer to refund the whole amount as store credit instead. Store credit refunds are instant.

## Cancelled orders
If you cancel an order you already paid for by card, the payment is refunded automatically. Store credit used on a cancelled order is returned to your balance.
