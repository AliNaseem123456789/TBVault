## Orders from several sellers
{{store_name}} is a marketplace. When your cart has items from different sellers, each seller packs and ships their own items, so one order can arrive in several packages. You pay shipping once per order, not per package. Each package has its own status and tracking number on the order page.

## Order statuses
- Pending: we have your order and the seller is preparing it.
- Confirmed: the seller has accepted it.
- Shipped: on its way. A tracking number is added when the seller has one.
- Delivered: it has arrived.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled before it shipped.
An order with several packages shows the status of the slowest package.

## Tracking an order
Signed-in customers see every order, with tracking details, under Orders in their account. Guests can use the Track order page with their order number and the email address used at checkout. You can also ask this assistant about your order when you are signed in.
