# Selling on TBVault

## Becoming a seller
Anyone with an account can apply on the Sell page with a store name and description. Our team reviews applications; once approved, you get a seller dashboard to add products, photos, options and stock.

## Fees
We keep a commission on each sale, currently {{commission_rate}}% of the item price by default (some sellers have a different agreed rate). Shipping, tax and coupons are covered by {{store_name}}, not by the seller.

## Orders and shipping
When a customer buys your products, the order appears in your seller dashboard. Confirm it, ship it, add the tracking number, and mark it delivered.

## Getting paid
Your earnings for an order are added to your balance once your part is delivered and the order is paid. Payouts are sent by our team and recorded in your dashboard. If an item is returned and refunded, its earnings are taken back from your balance.

## Suspension
We can suspend sellers who break our rules. While suspended, your products are hidden from the shop.
