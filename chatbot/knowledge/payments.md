# Payments

## Ways to pay
- Card: paid securely through Stripe at checkout. We never see or store your card number.
- Cash on delivery: pay when the order arrives. The order counts as paid once it is delivered.
- Store credit: use your balance for part or all of an order. If it doesn't cover everything, pay the rest by card or cash on delivery.

## Prices, tax and coupons
Prices are shown in US dollars. Sale prices are applied automatically. Tax is {{tax_rate}}% of the order after discounts; shipping is not taxed. Coupon codes are entered at checkout; each coupon has its own rules (minimum spend, expiry, how many times it can be used).

## If a card payment fails
Your order stays unpaid and you can try paying again from the order page, or cancel it while it hasn't shipped.
