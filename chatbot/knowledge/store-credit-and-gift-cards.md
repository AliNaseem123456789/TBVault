# Store credit and gift cards

## Store credit
Store credit is money held in your {{store_name}} account. See your balance and history on the Store credit (Wallet) page. You can use it at checkout for part or all of an order. You get store credit from refunds paid as credit, from gift cards, from top-ups, or from our team (for example as a goodwill gesture).

## Topping up
You can add between $5 and $1,000 of store credit at a time by card, from the Wallet page.

## Gift cards
Gift card codes look like TBV-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. Redeem one on the Wallet page; its full value is added to your store credit. Each code works once. Some gift cards have an expiry date; an expired or disabled code can't be redeemed.

## Can store credit be withdrawn?
No. Store credit can only be spent on {{store_name}}. It can't be turned back into cash.
