# Orders and cancellation

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order yourself while it is Pending or Confirmed, from the order page or by asking this assistant. Once any part of the order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled; you can return it after delivery instead.

## What happens when you cancel
- The items go back into stock.
- Card payments are refunded automatically to your card.
- Store credit you used is returned to your balance.
- Cash on delivery orders simply won't be delivered; nothing was paid.

## Changing an order
Orders can't be edited after they are placed. Cancel the order (if it hasn't shipped) and place a new one.

## Guest orders
You can check out without an account. Use the Track order page with your order number and email to see or cancel a guest order.
